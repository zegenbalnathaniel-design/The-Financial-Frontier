# The Financial Frontier

> Financial intelligence, reimagined in three dimensions.

The Financial Frontier is an interactive platform for exploring the global economy. Instead of static charts and dense tables, you navigate the data in 3D — spinning a live globe, tracing the connections between economies, and building your own comparisons — alongside a deep, fully-sourced report published every month.

**Educational use only — nothing here is investment advice.**

---

## What you can explore

### 🌍 The Intelligence Globe
An interactive 3D globe of the world's economies. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and recolour the whole map by any of 23 indicators — growth, inflation, interest rates, markets, and more. Click any hub to open a detailed panel for that economy.

### 🕸️ The Economic Network
A living map of how economies connect. Each node is a country; the links between them show shared trade blocs and groups. Filter by region, group, or connection type to see the shape of the global system.

### 📊 The Indicator Explorer
Build your own view of the data. Pick an indicator (23 of them, across 12 families), choose any set of economies, and see the result as a line chart, bar chart, data table, or live ranking — with a timeframe selector and one-click CSV export.

### 📈 The Macro Dashboard
The current global picture at a glance: growth forecasts, growth-versus-inflation, activity indices, and where the world's central banks are tightening, easing, or holding.

### 📰 The Monthly Report
A deep, fully-sourced briefing published every month, readable on-screen or downloadable as a PDF. Every figure is attributed — check the Sources panel on each issue.

### 📖 Glossary & Learn
Plain-language explanations of the terms and concepts behind the numbers, so the data is approachable whether or not you have a finance background.

---

## Getting around

From the homepage, use the module grid to jump straight into any experience, or use the navigation bar at the top of every page. The site works on desktop and mobile, and honours your system's reduced-motion setting if you prefer less animation.

---

## A note on the data

Transparency matters here, so it's worth knowing what's what:

- The **monthly reports** and the **macro dashboard** use real, attributed figures — every source is listed.
- The **3D globe**, **economic network**, and **indicator explorer** currently run on **illustrative sample data**, clearly labelled as such in the interface. They're designed to show what the platform can do; invented numbers are never presented as real.
